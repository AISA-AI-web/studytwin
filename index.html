STUDYTWIN

A student hub for the ADEK AI curriculum

Built for the American International School in Abu Dhabi. Students work through lessons and labs; teachers record attainment against the ADEK AI Fluency Framework.

What this repository is

Source code, not the platform

This repository holds the code. It is not where the platform runs and holds no student data of any kind.

StudyTwin is a Google Apps Script web app. It runs inside AISA's own Google Workspace, signs students in with their existing @aisa.sch.ae account, and stores every record in a private Google Sheet owned by the school. No student information leaves the school's own systems, and no third-party service processes it.

Attainment

Judged, not calculated

Attainment follows the ADEK K–12 AI Fluency Framework. Each of the four strands is judged by a teacher at Working towards, Emerging, Proficient or Advanced against the framework's own descriptors, drawing on what students make, what they do and what they say.

No percentage produces a level anywhere in the system. Worksheet marks are recorded as product evidence — one of the three sources a judgement draws on — and the overall level is computed by each grade's published decision rule.

Emerging is the expected standard in Term 1, not a low grade. A cohort sitting at Emerging is the framework working as designed.
